# PortfolioPro visual direction

No visual direction was specified by the user. Three distinct possibilities were considered; each is intentionally a tail-probability concept rather than a safe middle choice.

- **Night Terminal — 0.08.** A quiet, midnight-blue trading workstation with precise grid structure and a restrained mint signal color.
- **Paper Ledger — 0.04.** A warm, archival investment journal with cream paper, ink typography, and small engraved chart marks.
- **Signal Room — 0.06.** A high-energy charcoal control room with sharp lime signals, hard-edged panels, and dense technical overlays.

## Selected: Night Terminal

**Design movement:** Contemporary financial terminal, softened with editorial spacing rather than imitating legacy broker software.

**Core principles:** Calm, legible, trustworthy, information-dense but not crowded; clear separation between observed sample data and simulated actions.

**Color philosophy:** Deep blue-black canvas; layered slate surfaces; cool gray secondary text; mint for positive movement and primary actions; coral for negative movement; amber for caution. Never rely on color alone to indicate direction or risk.

**Layout paradigm:** Persistent left rail on desktop, compact top controls, wide content canvas, responsive stacked cards at narrow widths. Use clear section headers, bounded data tables, and chart panels with visible labels.

**Signature elements:** Small chart-grid texture, compact market-session badge, tabular-number figures, subtle sparklines, rounded-square monogram tokens, and an explicit DEMO / PAPER MODE status.

**Interaction philosophy:** Fast direct navigation, one clear primary action per view, inline filters, accessible keyboard focus, and explicit order review before simulation. Order success is shown only after the simulated action is accepted.

**Animation:** Restrained transitions for tabs, dialogs, and chart ranges; no looping or decorative motion.

**Typography system:** Native/system sans-serif for interface copy; tabular numerals for prices and quantities; compact uppercase labels for metadata; no remote font dependency.

**Brand essence:** A composed co-pilot for deliberate investing.

**Brand voice:** Clear, measured, transparent about uncertainty and demo data; no claims of guaranteed returns.

**Wordmark/logo:** “PortfolioPro” in sentence case. Icon is a simple, rising three-bar market mark on a solid midnight-blue square, with a mint foreground and comfortable clear space.

**Signature brand color:** Mint green `#75E2B5`, used sparingly on dark slate.
